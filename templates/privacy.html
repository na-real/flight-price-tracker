TRANSPARENCY & DATA PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This platform is engineered with privacy-first principles. We collect and retain only the minimal information required to execute flight searches and deliver automated price alerts.

1. Information We Process

When you perform flight searches on SkyTrack, we process your requested departure airport, arrival airport, travel dates, and cabin class to query configured flight aggregators (Google Flights via SerpAPI) and calculate multi-website price comparisons. Search queries are not tied to personal identities.

2. Tracked Flight Radar & Email Alerts

If you elect to monitor a flight fare via Price Radar, your specified route, target price threshold, and recipient email address are stored in our local database. Your email is exclusively utilized to transmit automated notifications when a flight fare drops to or below your threshold. We never sell, lease, or distribute your email address to advertisers or marketers.

3. External Flight Data Providers

Flight prices, itineraries, and booking links are retrieved via authenticated APIs and official carrier channels. When you click an external booking button, you are redirected to the selected provider (such as an airline official portal or travel booking platform) governed by their individual terms and privacy policies.

4. Data Control & Deletion

You maintain total ownership of your tracked routes. You may immediately delete any tracked route and associated alert email from our system using the delete action on the Price Watch dashboard.

5. Security

We employ strict security practices, including HTTP security headers (nosniff, frame protection, referrer policies), parameterized database queries to prevent injection vulnerabilities, and server-side secret management.
