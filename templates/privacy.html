DISCRETION & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

SkyTracker is engineered with privacy-first principles. We collect and retain only the minimal operational data required to execute flight searches and deliver autonomous price alerts.

1. Route Search Processing

When you perform flight searches on SkyTracker, we process your requested departure airport, arrival airport, travel dates, and cabin class to query verified flight aggregators (Google Flights via SerpAPI) and compute multi-provider price comparisons. Search queries are anonymous and never tied to personal identities.

2. Autonomous Price Radar & Dispatch

If you elect to monitor a flight fare via Price Radar, your specified route, target price threshold, and recipient email address are stored in our encrypted database. Your email is exclusively utilized to transmit automated notifications when a flight fare reaches or drops below your target. We never sell, lease, or distribute your email address to advertisers or marketers.

3. External Flight Data Providers

Flight prices, schedules, and booking links are retrieved via authenticated APIs and official carrier channels. When you select an external booking offer, you are redirected directly to the provider (airline direct portal or authorized travel channel) governed by their individual terms and privacy policies.

4. Complete Data Ownership & Immediate Deletion

You maintain total ownership of your saved routes. You may delete any tracked flight and purge your notification record at any moment using the delete control on the Price Watch dashboard or by supplying your deletion token.

5. Security Engineering

We employ strict security practices, including HTTP security headers (nosniff, frame protection, strict referrer policies), parameterized database queries to eliminate injection vulnerabilities, and server-side secret management.
